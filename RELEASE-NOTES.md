## ForeverCraft 0.1.134

- **Finance charts in status-bar style:** bars use Blizzard's status bar texture with a lighter top edge, darker bottom edge and fine outline in muted colours (emerald income, ruby expenses, gold total wealth, blue bag value, violet bank value, yellow-gold cash). Horizontal bars sit in ForeverCraft's recessed progress track.
- **Income sources / expense categories:** each row shows icon, name and amount on top, the bar with the percentage below; 100 % fills the whole track, amounts never overlap the bar, long names get the full row width.
- **Time charts:** bars of one width with outline and mouse-over highlight, readable date labels, value axis whose width follows the longest amount (no more cut labels).
- **Frames:** content panels use a nine-slice border (corners keep their size) around the professions background texture – no distorted or cut corners on small panels. Every chart and the key figures have a professions-style section header; “Most valuable items” has its own header.

## ForeverCraft 0.1.131

- **Redesign:** the professions side tabs now form one compact navigation rail with tooltips (module, purpose, key binding, why something is unavailable). The finance window was redesigned: Blizzard top tabs, a shared filter bar with dropdowns, a slimmer character list with gold per character, large wealth and money-flow tiles, framed charts with guide lines, a key figure panel with the most valuable items, category bars with icons, a striped transaction list with column headers, empty-state hints and a status bar (price source, missing prices, data age, money check). Deleting finance data moved to the settings and the character right-click.
- **Finances:** `/fcfinance` or the coin side tab in the professions window. ForeverCraft records every change of your money in the background (quests, loot, vendor, auction house, trade, mail, trainers, repairs, flight master, bank and more) – exactly once, also across reloads and logins. Money that cannot be assigned reliably is stored as “other”, never guessed; categories can be corrected by hand without changing the amount. Transfers between your own characters are kept out of income and expenses. Overview tiles (cash, bag/bank value, total wealth, income, expenses, balance), an income/expense chart, wealth per character with a history chart, income and expense breakdowns and a searchable transaction list – for one or all characters and any period. Bag and bank value use a selectable valuation (best realistic sale, vendor, auction market, auction net, disenchant); items without a price are counted separately, never as 0.
- **Bank view:** `/fcbank` or the chest side tab in the professions window opens a ForeverCraft view showing the saved bank of every character on this account – anywhere, without visiting a banker. Bank tabs are shown as real slots with item icons, quality borders and stack sizes. Switch characters (class icon and colour) or view all banks combined, search by name and filter by category (cloth, ore & bars, herbs, leather, enchanting materials, weapons, armor, recipes, consumables, quest items, soulbound, tradeable) or quality, and sort by layout, name, quality, quantity, value or category. Every bank shows when it was last updated, how many slots are used and its estimated value.
- **Item stock tooltip:** item tooltips list how many of an item each of your characters carries in bags and bank, plus the total.
- **Crafting planning:** missing materials in the shopping list and material planner now show how many lie on your bank or on other characters. These stay listed as missing, because only items in your bags can be used for crafting.
- **Settings → Bank:** turn bank/bag recording, the stock tooltip and the shopping-list hint on or off, open the bank view and reset saved data (with confirmation). Single characters can be removed from the bank view.
- Bank and bag contents of other characters are saved snapshots, not live data. The bank is only recorded while it is open, so an inaccessible bank is never saved as empty.
