## ForeverCraft 0.1.136

- **Profession links for every profession:** the client only returns the profession link of the profession window that is open. ForeverCraft now records the link of each profession when you open it, so Tailoring, Enchanting and every other crafting profession get their clickable link in the offer, whichever window is open. A profession that has never been opened falls back to its name.
- **Recipe picker for all selected professions:** new window with search, profession and category filters, recipes grouped by profession and collapsible categories, selection count per profession, item/recipe tooltips, “Clear selection” and “Use selection”. Recipes of each profession are recorded when its window is opened (new recipes are picked up automatically) and stay available afterwards; a profession that was not recorded yet shows a clear hint. Views of other players' professions are never recorded.
- **Enchantments:** offered as a service with their enchant link; bind-on-pickup crafting results stay excluded.
- **Message length:** visible length and technical length (including link codes, which count for the 255-byte limit) are shown separately. “Suggest a shorter version” tries a shorter wording, drops the maximum skill, replaces links by names and leaves out the whisper request – professions are never removed and links never cut. Recipes now read “I can also make …, among other things.”
- **Layout:** the options in the offer window are listed in one column with full labels.
- **Offer buttons in the crafting log:** “Configure offer” opens the offer window, “Offer crafting” posts the saved offer (active profile, channel, language and recipes) with one click. Its subtitle shows whether it is ready, the remaining cooldown, a missing trade chat or that the offer was posted; the tooltip shows the message, channel and profile, and a right-click offers profiles, preview and post. Nothing is posted without that click. The title bar button is gone; the profession guides button stays in the title bar.
- **Two messages:** an offer that is too long for one chat line is split into two messages at a sentence or word boundary (links are never cut) and both are sent in the same click. The length display shows the number of messages; the offer counts as confirmed only when both lines appeared in the chat. Offers that need more than two messages are blocked.
- **Fixed:** a successfully posted offer was reported as “did not appear in the chat”. The server echoes links with its own link code, so the check now compares the visible text.

## ForeverCraft 0.1.135

- **Offer crafting in the trade chat:** new button “Offer crafting” in the title bar of the professions window (next to the profession guides). Pick the professions of this character (gathering professions are not preselected), set terms for all of them or per profession (customer's mats, voluntary tip, tip, fixed fee in gold/silver/copper per craft or per order, own mats, mats by arrangement, free, leveling, custom terms) and optionally add special recipes of the open profession with search and categories.
- Recipes whose crafted item binds on pickup cannot be offered (checked with the bind type of the crafted item and a hidden tooltip); enchantments are offered as a service. Recipe cooldowns are shown when the client reports them.
- Natural message templates (“Casual and friendly”, “Short and direct”) in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Traditional Chinese; the message language can differ from the client language. Own templates with placeholders ({berufe}, {berufe_skill}, {rezepte}, {konditionen}, {verguetung}, {charakter}, {berufslinks}, {rezeptlinks}); lines with an empty placeholder are dropped, no raw placeholder ever reaches the chat.
- Editable preview with length counter; messages over 255 characters are blocked (links are never cut). Trade, guild, general or custom channels, found by name instead of number; if the trade chat is not available, nothing is sent elsewhere. Optional confirmation, local waiting time (60/120/180/300 s) with countdown on the button, and a check that the message really appeared in the chat.
- Offer profiles per character, right-click menu with the profiles, optional quick posting with Shift + left-click (off by default). New settings page “Crafting offers”. Every post needs a click – no timed, repeated or automatic messages.

## ForeverCraft 0.1.131

- **Redesign:** the professions side tabs now form one compact navigation rail with tooltips (module, purpose, key binding, why something is unavailable). The finance window was redesigned: Blizzard top tabs, a shared filter bar with dropdowns, a slimmer character list with gold per character, large wealth and money-flow tiles, framed charts with guide lines, a key figure panel with the most valuable items, category bars with icons, a striped transaction list with column headers, empty-state hints and a status bar (price source, missing prices, data age, money check). Deleting finance data moved to the settings and the character right-click.
- **Finances:** `/fcfinance` or the coin side tab in the professions window. ForeverCraft records every change of your money in the background (quests, loot, vendor, auction house, trade, mail, trainers, repairs, flight master, bank and more) – exactly once, also across reloads and logins. Money that cannot be assigned reliably is stored as “other”, never guessed; categories can be corrected by hand without changing the amount. Transfers between your own characters are kept out of income and expenses. Overview tiles (cash, bag/bank value, total wealth, income, expenses, balance), an income/expense chart, wealth per character with a history chart, income and expense breakdowns and a searchable transaction list – for one or all characters and any period. Bag and bank value use a selectable valuation (best realistic sale, vendor, auction market, auction net, disenchant); items without a price are counted separately, never as 0.
- **Bank view:** `/fcbank` or the chest side tab in the professions window opens a ForeverCraft view showing the saved bank of every character on this account – anywhere, without visiting a banker. Bank tabs are shown as real slots with item icons, quality borders and stack sizes. Switch characters (class icon and colour) or view all banks combined, search by name and filter by category (cloth, ore & bars, herbs, leather, enchanting materials, weapons, armor, recipes, consumables, quest items, soulbound, tradeable) or quality, and sort by layout, name, quality, quantity, value or category. Every bank shows when it was last updated, how many slots are used and its estimated value.
- **Item stock tooltip:** item tooltips list how many of an item each of your characters carries in bags and bank, plus the total.
- **Crafting planning:** missing materials in the shopping list and material planner now show how many lie on your bank or on other characters. These stay listed as missing, because only items in your bags can be used for crafting.
- **Settings → Bank:** turn bank/bag recording, the stock tooltip and the shopping-list hint on or off, open the bank view and reset saved data (with confirmation). Single characters can be removed from the bank view.
- Bank and bag contents of other characters are saved snapshots, not live data. The bank is only recorded while it is open, so an inaccessible bank is never saved as empty.
